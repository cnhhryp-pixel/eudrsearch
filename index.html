Scope overview

Seven EUDR commodity families

Coverage is determined by the products listed in Annex I, not by commodity name alone.

01

Cattle

Selected cattle products and certain derived products.

02

Cocoa

Cocoa beans, paste, butter, powder, chocolate and listed derivatives.

03

Coffee

Coffee products, including newly updated product categories.

04

Oil palm

Palm oil and listed derivatives in the current Annex I.

05

Rubber

Natural rubber and selected derived rubber products.

06

Soya

Soya beans, flour, meal, oil and listed derivatives.

07

Wood

Wood, furniture, paper and other listed wood-derived products.

Practical workflow

From product screening to due diligence

1

Confirm scope

Match the product against the current Annex I CN classification.

2

Collect origin data

Gather supplier, production, quantity and geolocation information.

3

Assess risk

Evaluate deforestation, legality and supply-chain risk using required evidence.

4

Mitigate when needed

Obtain additional information or take proportionate mitigation measures.

5

Submit / retain evidence

Prepare the required EUDR declaration or due-diligence record and retain supporting evidence.
