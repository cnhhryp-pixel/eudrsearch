Palm Oil & EUDR

Check EUDR scope and due-diligence considerations for palm oil and listed palm-derived products.

Scope

Oil palm is a core EUDR commodity family. Coverage follows the products and derivatives listed in the current Annex I rather than every product containing palm-based material.

2026 update

Some palm-oil derivative categories were added in the 2026 scope update. Verify the current CN classification before relying on an older compliance matrix.

Scope reminder

Verify the exact current Annex I CN entry and your transaction facts before treating a product as in or out of scope.
