EUDR Rubber Requirements

Natural rubber and specified derived products are covered by EUDR, while the 2026 scope update also removed some rubber-derived categories.

STEP 1

Confirm scope

Because not every rubber product is covered, exact customs classification is particularly important. Separate natural-rubber scope analysis from synthetic-rubber assumptions.

STEP 2

Map origin

Identify production country, suppliers and the production plots or other required location data connected to the covered commodity.

STEP 3

Assess risk

Check the country benchmarking category and assess the evidence needed to support deforestation-free and legality requirements.
