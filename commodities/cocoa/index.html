EUDR Cocoa Requirements

Cocoa beans and listed derivatives such as cocoa paste, butter, powder and chocolate sit at the centre of EUDR scope.

STEP 1

Confirm scope

Chocolate and processed cocoa can still be in scope even when the cocoa is only one ingredient. Confirm the exact CN heading and preserve traceability back through suppliers to production plots.

STEP 2

Map origin

Identify production country, suppliers and the production plots or other required location data connected to the covered commodity.

STEP 3

Assess risk

Check the country benchmarking category and assess the evidence needed to support deforestation-free and legality requirements.
