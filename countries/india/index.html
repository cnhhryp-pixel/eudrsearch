Re-check the latest official EU implementing act before relying on the classification for a transaction.

Why this country matters

India participates in multiple EUDR-relevant commodity and processed-product supply chains, making product-code and origin checks important.

Scope still comes first

Country risk does not determine whether a product is covered. First confirm the current Annex I product entry and exact CN classification.

Build the evidence trail

Keep supplier, production-origin, geolocation, legality and internal review records appropriate to the applicable EUDR obligations.
