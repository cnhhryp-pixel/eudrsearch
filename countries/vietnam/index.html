What the category means

Country benchmarking changes how the EUDR due-diligence framework is applied. It does not automatically make a shipment compliant or non-compliant.

Product scope still comes first

Confirm that the product is listed in Annex I and determine the exact CN code before building the due-diligence file.

Keep evidence

Maintain classification, supplier, origin, geolocation and internal review records appropriate to the applicable obligations.
