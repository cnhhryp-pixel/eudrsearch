EUDR Deadlines 2026–2027

The main application dates changed. Use the current timetable rather than older 2024 or 2025 implementation calendars.

30 DEC 2026

Large & medium operators

Main EUDR obligations apply. Micro and small operators already covered by the former EU Timber Regulation also fall into this date.

30 JUN 2027

Other micro & small operators

Main application date for other micro and small operators.

30 DEC 2027

Newly added 2026 products

Products newly added by Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/2102, including soluble coffee, certain palm-oil derivatives and frozen cattle tongues, become subject from this date.

Do not use an old deadline article

EUDR dates were postponed more than once. Always check the current consolidated legislation and Commission implementation page.
