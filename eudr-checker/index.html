EUDR Product Checker

Screen a product by name, commodity or common CN/HS heading before you move into full due diligence.

What this tool does

It gives an initial scope signal. The legally decisive step is checking the exact product against the current Annex I.

1. Identify the exact product

Use the commercial description together with the customs classification. Similar products can have different EUDR treatment depending on their CN code and material composition.

2. Confirm the commodity family

EUDR covers cattle, cocoa, coffee, oil palm, rubber, soya and wood, plus specified derived products.

3. Check timing

The main application dates are 30 December 2026 and 30 June 2027, with separate timing for products newly added to scope in 2026.
