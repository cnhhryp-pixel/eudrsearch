Country benchmarking

EUDR Country Risk Checker

Search a country to see the current EUDR benchmarking category used for the due-diligence framework.

Three-tier system

Countries are treated as low, standard or high risk. Countries not listed as low or high retain standard risk.

Low risk examples

China, Vietnam, United States, India, EU Member States and many others are on the current low-risk list.

Standard risk examples

Brazil and Indonesia are examples of countries that are not in the low/high Annex list and therefore retain standard risk.

High risk

Belarus, Democratic People’s Republic of Korea, Myanmar and Russian Federation are listed as high risk.

Important

Benchmarking changes the compliance approach and authority-check rates, but operators still need to meet the applicable EUDR requirements. Re-check the official list before a transaction because classifications can be revised.
