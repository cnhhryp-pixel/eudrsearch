Pricing FAQ

Common questions

Is €49 a subscription?

No. Professional is a one-time payment for one assessment pack / scenario.

Do I need Professional to use the checker?

No. The Combined Checker, product database, CN references and country-risk tools remain free.

What counts as one assessment scenario?

One primary product or CN classification combined with one main production-country / supply scenario. Complex multi-product or multi-country work is better handled as Enterprise.

Can I use the report as legal advice?

No. The report is a structured screening and compliance-preparation record. Final legal decisions should be verified against current EU law, official guidance and professional advice where needed.
