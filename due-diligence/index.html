EUDR Due Diligence Checklist

A working checklist for organising the information, assessment and evidence needed for an EUDR compliance file.

Information

Build a traceable product file: product description, quantity, supplier, production country and the required geolocation and production information.

Risk assessment

Assess whether there is a risk that the product is not deforestation-free or was not produced in accordance with relevant legislation.

Risk mitigation

If risk is not negligible, gather additional evidence, perform verification or take other proportionate measures before proceeding.
