EUDR Due Diligence Statement Guide

Understand where the DDS fits in the compliance workflow and what information should be ready before submission.

Before the statement

The statement is not the start of due diligence. Product scope, traceability information, risk assessment and any necessary mitigation should already be organised.

Information System

The EU Information System is the operational environment used for due-diligence statement workflows. In 2026 the Commission adopted additional measures simplifying submission, including provisions for simplified declarations for eligible micro or small primary operators.

Internal recordkeeping

Keep the DDS reference together with supplier evidence, geolocation, classification work, risk assessment and approval records so the transaction can be reconstructed later.
