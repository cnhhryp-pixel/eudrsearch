Bamboo Furniture and EUDR

Bamboo furniture should not automatically be treated as EUDR wood furniture.

Material excluded

Bamboo and rattan are not treated as EUDR wood commodities under the updated scope.

Key rule

The updated wood entries exclude products made from bamboo, rattan and other excluded materials of a woody nature.

Common mistake

Do not classify a product as EUDR wood merely because it is furniture or because the material looks woody.
