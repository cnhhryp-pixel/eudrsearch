Wooden Kitchenware & EUDR

Wooden tableware and kitchenware can fall within EUDR scope under ex 4419.

Primary CN reference

ex 4419

Status

Potential EUDR wood-product match

Next step

Confirm exact classification, material and transaction facts in the combined checker.

Examples

Wooden boards, utensils and similar kitchen items can remain relevant EUDR products even though they are highly processed consumer goods.

Material matters

Bamboo/rattan items should not be treated as EUDR wood. Used and second-hand products are excluded.
