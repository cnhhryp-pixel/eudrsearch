Wooden Box & EUDR

Wooden boxes can be covered when marketed as products themselves, while boxes used solely as packaging for another product can be excluded.

Primary CN reference

ex 4415

Status

Conditional — depends on commercial use

Key question

Are you selling the wooden box itself, or is the box only carrying another product?

Why the distinction matters

The 2026 wording makes packaging-use exclusions explicit for single-use and clearly reusable packing containers.
