Wooden Frame & EUDR

Wooden picture, photo and mirror frames can fall under ex 4414 when made from covered wood.

Primary CN reference

ex 4414

Status

Finished consumer product

Even a finished decorative item can remain an EUDR relevant wood product if its Annex I classification applies.

Exclusions

Used/second-hand products are excluded; bamboo/rattan frames should not be treated as EUDR wood.
