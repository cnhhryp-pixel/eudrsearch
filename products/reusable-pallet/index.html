Reusable Wooden Pallet & EUDR

A reusable pallet may be an EUDR product when sold as a pallet, but can fall outside scope from the moment it is actually used exclusively to carry another marketed/exported product and presented with that product.

Primary CN reference

ex 4415

Status

Conditional — stage of use matters

Key question

Is the packaging itself being sold, or is it already being used exclusively to carry another product?

Timeline matters

Before first use

A reusable pallet sold as a product in its own right can fall under ex 4415.

From packaging use onwards

Clearly reusable packing can be excluded once it is used exclusively to support, protect or carry another product and is presented with that product.

Used / second-hand

Used and second-hand products are separately excluded by the updated Annex I wording.
