MDF & EUDR

MDF is generally screened under the fibreboard entry. Exact material, CN classification and current used-product exclusions still need to be checked.

Why MDF matters

MDF is widely used in furniture, cabinetry and construction. A highly processed board can still remain a relevant wood product under Annex I.

Material matters

Confirm the board is made from material within the EUDR wood commodity definition; bamboo/rattan should not be treated as EUDR wood.
