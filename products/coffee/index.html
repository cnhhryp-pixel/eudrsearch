Coffee & EUDR

Check whether coffee products may fall under EUDR and what traceability evidence businesses should prepare.

Commodity family

Coffee

Scope screening

Coffee is one of the seven core EUDR commodity families. Current scope checks should distinguish green/roasted coffee from newly added soluble-coffee categories and their applicable dates.

Code reference

Common references include heading 0901; exact CN subheadings should be verified.

What businesses should prepare

Start with the exact CN classification, then map the relevant supplier and production origin. For covered products, prepare the geolocation, legality and risk-assessment evidence required for the applicable due-diligence workflow.
