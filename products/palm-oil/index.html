Palm Oil & EUDR

Check EUDR scope and due-diligence considerations for palm oil and relevant palm-derived products.

Commodity family

Oil palm

Scope screening

Oil palm is one of the seven core EUDR commodities. Certain palm derivatives were added by the 2026 scope update and have their own application timing.

Code reference

Heading 1511 is a common palm-oil reference; derivative classifications vary.

What businesses should prepare

Start with the exact CN classification, then map the relevant supplier and production origin. For covered products, prepare the geolocation, legality and risk-assessment evidence required for the applicable due-diligence workflow.
