Single-Use Wooden Pallet & EUDR

A single-use pallet sold as a product can fall within ex 4415, while a pallet used exclusively to support, protect or carry another product and presented with that product can be excluded.

Primary CN reference

ex 4415

Status

Conditional — product vs packing use matters

Key question

Is the packaging itself being sold, or is it already being used exclusively to carry another product?

Typical export case

A pallet purchased and then used to ship machinery or cartons may be treated differently from a pallet shipment where the pallets themselves are the goods being sold.

Commercial use controls the outcome

Do not determine EUDR scope from the pallet CN code alone.
