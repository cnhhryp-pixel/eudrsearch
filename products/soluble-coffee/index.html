Soluble Coffee and EUDR

Soluble coffee was added to EUDR Annex I in the 2026 product-scope update.

Added in 2026

Special date: 30 December 2027 for newly added products.

Code reference

HS/CN 2101 11 00 is the key reference cited in the 2026 amendment.

Application date

Newly added products become subject to EUDR from 30 December 2027.

Commodity

Coffee.

What changed?

Roasted and green coffee were already in the EUDR framework. The 2026 update closed a gap by adding soluble coffee.
