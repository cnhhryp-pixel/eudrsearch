Wooden Flooring & EUDR

Assembled wooden flooring panels can fall within the builders' joinery/carpentry entry under ex 4418.

Primary CN reference

ex 4418

Status

Potential EUDR wood-product match

Examples

Mosaic and multilayer assembled flooring panels can fall within relevant 4418 subheadings where material conditions are met.

Material and condition matter

Used/second-hand products are excluded, and bamboo/rattan flooring should not be treated as EUDR wood.
