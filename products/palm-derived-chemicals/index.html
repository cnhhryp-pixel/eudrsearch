2026 scope update

Palm-Derived Chemicals and EUDR

The 2026 Annex I amendment added selected palm-derived oleochemical and chemical entries.

Added in 2026

Exact CN classification is essential because the new entries are highly specific.

Examples

Added entries include selected products under ex 1516 20, ex 1518 00, ex 1520 00, ex 2916 15, ex 2921 19, ex 2923 90 00, ex 3824 99 and others.

Condition

Coverage depends on the exact CN entry, whether the product is produced using oil palm, and any stated exemption.

Application date

These newly added products generally apply from 30 December 2027.
