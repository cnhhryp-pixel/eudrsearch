Wooden Pallet & EUDR

A wooden pallet can be in EUDR scope when traded as a product in its own right, but a pallet used only to support, protect or carry another product can be excluded when the Annex I conditions are met.

Primary CN reference

ex 4415

Status

Conditional — depends on how the pallet is placed on the market

Next step

Confirm product composition, transaction use and origin-country facts in the combined checker.

Two different outcomes

Pallet sold as the product

If the pallet itself is placed on the market or exported as a product in its own right, the 4415 wood-product entry can apply.

Pallet carrying another product

Specified packing used exclusively to support, protect or carry another product can fall outside EUDR when presented/used with that product.

Used pallets

The 2026 Annex I wording also excludes used and second-hand products.

Do not decide from HS 4415 alone

Commercial use of the pallet is part of the scope analysis.
