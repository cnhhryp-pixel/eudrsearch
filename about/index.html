About

EUDRSearch

An independent screening and reference website built to make EUDR product scope, country benchmarking and compliance preparation easier to navigate.

What EUDRSearch does

EUDRSearch combines product and CN/HS-code screening, country-risk references, application-date logic and practical due-diligence guidance. The site is designed for importers, exporters, manufacturers and supply-chain teams that need a fast starting point before checking the official legal text.

What it does not do

EUDRSearch is not an EU institution, competent authority or law firm. Automated checker results are informational screening outputs, not binding legal determinations or proof of compliance.

How information is maintained

Core regulatory pages are checked against European Commission and EUR-Lex sources. When product scope, application timing or country benchmarking changes, the goal is to update the affected checker logic and reference pages together.
