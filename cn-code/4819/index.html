ex 4819 — Paper cartons, boxes, cases and packing containers

EUDR reference for cartons, boxes, cases, bags and other packing containers of paper or paperboard.

Commodity

Wood / paper

Status

Covered Chapter 48 entry with exclusions

Timing

Main EUDR date based on operator size

Why this entry matters

This is one of the most commercially important paper headings because product-vs-packaging use changes the EUDR outcome.

2026 Annex I exclusions

Specified packaging used exclusively to support, protect or carry another marketed/exported product can be excluded when the stated conditions are met.
