4007 — Vulcanised rubber thread and cord

EUDR reference for vulcanised rubber thread and cord made from covered natural rubber.

Commodity

Rubber

Material condition

Screen only where the product is made from the EUDR rubber commodity, i.e. natural rubber from Hevea brasiliensis.

Timing

Main EUDR date based on operator size

Why this entry matters

This is a specific downstream rubber heading and should be distinguished from broader rubber articles removed from scope in 2026.
