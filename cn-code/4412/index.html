ex 4412 — Plywood, veneered panels and laminated wood

EUDR scope reference for plywood, veneered panels and similar laminated wood under ex 4412.

Commodity

Wood

Status

Covered with used-product exclusion

Timing

Main EUDR date based on operator size

Why this entry matters

Plywood is a common downstream wood product where product-to-origin traceability can become complex across multiple veneer layers and suppliers.

2026 Annex I exclusions

Used and second-hand products are excluded. Confirm that the material is EUDR wood and not an excluded material such as bamboo/rattan.
