ex 4804 — Uncoated kraft paper and paperboard

EUDR reference for uncoated kraft paper and paperboard within ex Chapter 48.

Commodity

Wood / paper

Status

Covered Chapter 48 entry with exclusions

Timing

Main EUDR date based on operator size

Why this entry matters

Kraft paper is a major packaging and industrial paper product, but use conditions can change the EUDR outcome.

2026 Annex I exclusions

Waste, used/second-hand and recovered products are excluded. Packaging used exclusively to support/protect/carry another product can also be excluded in specified circumstances.
